AI tool prompts used in this project
====================================
Tool: Claude (Anthropic)

[Project Planning & Benchmark Selection]
- Prompt: "Review the 10 approved pyperformance workloads provided in the 
  project instructions and recommend the two best benchmarks to analyze. 
  Focus on selecting benchmarks that offer distinct performance bottlenecks 
  and serve as strong, feasible candidates for eventual hardware 
  acceleration."
- Purpose of AI Use: The AI was utilized to evaluate the project's 
  approved list of pyperformance benchmarks and filter the options based 
  on their underlying algorithms and data structures. This 
  helped identify nbody and raytrace as the most strategic choices for 
  detecting optimizations and designing hardware modules.

[Bottleneck Analysis]
- Prompt: "I have executed the chosen benchmarks on the QEMU VM using 
  python3-dbg and generated the corresponding perf reports and flame 
  graphs. Can you analyze this profiling data to pinpoint the primary 
  performance hotspots and explain why these specific Python functions 
  are consuming excessive resources?"
- Purpose of AI Use: The AI acted as an analytical aid to interpret the 
  complex runtime behavior visualized in the flame graphs and stack 
  traces. It helped translate the raw hardware performance 
  counters and Python internal calls into actionable insights regarding 
  inefficient code blocks.

[Reporting & Output]
- Prompt: "Help me write a shell script to automate the pyperformance 
  execution for both the baseline and optimized code. The script needs to 
  cleanly format and print the comparison results to clearly demonstrate 
  whether the required 7% performance improvement was achieved for the 
  final text report."
- Purpose of AI Use: The AI was tasked with structuring the execution 
  scripts and organizing the display of the performance data. 
  This ensured the final benchmark reports properly captured the metrics 
  and clearly highlighted the execution time reductions in a consistent, 
  readable format.

[Hardware Architecture Diagram]
- Prompt: "Generate a picture or block diagram to visually explain how 
  the flow of the hardware works. Show the data paths and how the 
  proposed hardware accelerator interfaces with the existing software."
- Purpose of AI Use: The AI was used to help conceptualize and generate 
  a visual representation of the hardware architecture. This 
  clarified the internal logic and system integration, assisting in the 
  creation of the required hardware block diagram for the final report.
